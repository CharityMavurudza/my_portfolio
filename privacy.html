Privacy First.

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

As a Cyber Security specialist, I believe privacy is a fundamental human right. This portfolio is designed with a "Privacy First" architecture, meaning I collect the absolute minimum amount of data required to communicate with you.

2. Data Collection

I do not use invasive cookies or third-party trackers (like Google Analytics). The only data collected is:

  • Contact Information: Name, email, and message content if you choose to use the contact form.
  • Theme Preference: A small local storage key is used to remember if you prefer Dark or Light mode. This stays on your device.

3. Data Usage & Security

Your data is never sold or shared for marketing purposes. Contact form submissions are handled via Formspree and are protected by industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption during transmission.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of any communication we have had. Simply reach out via LinkedIn or WhatsApp to initiate a data wipe.
